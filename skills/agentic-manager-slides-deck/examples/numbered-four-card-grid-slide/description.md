# Numbered Four-Card Grid

**Use for:** four numbered items of equal weight, each a short title and a paragraph, such as themes, questions or principles; two such slides make a set of eight.

**Layout.** An uppercase eyebrow with a short teal rule, then a large title, at top left. Below them a two-by-two grid of equal rounded cards fills the rest of the slide, with a gap between them. Each card has a small navy square badge with its number, a bold heading beside it, then a paragraph of three to five lines. Date at top right, page number, footer and logo at the bottom.

**Style.** White background, white cards with a thin light grey border, navy badges with white numbers, navy headings, grey body text, teal eyebrow.

**Density.** Heavy: about 250 words.
