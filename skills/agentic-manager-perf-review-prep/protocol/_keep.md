The review needs the picture over the whole period, not every detail. Keep only what has a clear impact or is a significant signal about the person; a small thing seen once, such as a minor slip, a one-off thanks or a routine task, stays out unless it repeats into a pattern, which is then one point. Leave out what only names the person in passing, and praise addressed to a team they're in, which isn't theirs even when they worked on what it praises: what the person did in the same note or conversation counts as anything else. When nothing is worth keeping, submit every part empty.

For example:

| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Praise for the release Jane worked on, addressed to the squad: "the squad did a great job" | Leave out the praise; the rest of the note or conversation counts as any other | Praise addressed to the team isn't Jane's     |
| Sam thanking Jane by name, once, for a quick answer                                        | Leave out                                                                      | A one-off thanks for a small thing, seen once |
