The review needs the picture over the whole period, not every detail. Keep only what has a clear impact or is a significant signal about the person; a small thing seen once, such as a minor slip, a one-off thanks or a routine task, stays out unless it repeats into a pattern, which is then one point. Leave out what only names the person in passing, and praise addressed to a team they're in, which isn't theirs even when they worked on what it praises: what the person did in the same note or conversation counts as anything else.
