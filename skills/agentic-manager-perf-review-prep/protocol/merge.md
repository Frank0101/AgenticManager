# Merge what a manager's notes say about a person

A manager is preparing a performance review of the person named below. Other agents read the manager's notes a month at a time, and the person's pages, and found the items below, each one point from one note, with its day; they also listed the notes they left out in doubt. Merge the items into one summary of what the notes say about the person.

## How to merge

- Points about the same behaviour or outcome become one point, with every day it was seen on in `dates`, oldest first. Write a pattern as one: "Didn't update the rollout's status, many times", not one point per day. Keep apart points that are about different things, even when they share a value.
- Keep every point the items make, add none they don't, and keep each in its part. When items about the same thing disagree, keep both as points, or say how it changed, such as "Shared progress rarely in July, every week from September".
- A merged point takes the value of the items it merges, or, when they differ, the one it shows most clearly.
- `context` points are sentences alone: no value, no dates. Merge context that says the same thing into one.
- When the items are none, submit every part empty.
- A note left out in doubt can become a point only when an item or another note settles the doubt, such as one month's "good specs" with no author and another's "Jane's documents" naming the same specs. Then add the point with that note's day, and list it in `promoted`: the in-doubt line, copied exactly as listed, and what settled it. Leave every other note left out in doubt as it is.

## Examples

| Items                                                                                                         | Merged                                                                           | Why                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Jane works on everything, with high throughput" (Sep), "Jane adds scope nobody asked for" (Oct)              | One point, both days                                                             | The same behaviour: how much Jane takes on                                        |
| "Jane works on everything, with high throughput" (Sep), "Jane was held up to colleagues as the example" (Oct) | Two points                                                                       | Related, but different things: what Jane does, and how others are pointed to Jane |
| "Jane didn't flag the delay until asked" (Jul), "Jane flags risks early now" (Oct)                            | One point, both days: "Flagged delays only when asked in July, early by October" | The same behaviour, changed over time                                             |
