A `going_well` or `to_improve` point names every company value below that it clearly shows, at most two, judged from what it says and each value's meaning, or none when none fits, the one it shows most first. Consider every value, not only the most common one: a point about delivering a new way of working can show both delivery and challenging how things are done. Never stretch a point to fit a value. A `context` point names none.
