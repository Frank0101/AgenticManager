# List the person's work before the period

A manager is preparing a performance review of the person named below, which looks back over the whole year. The report ends its work and impact with a short reminder of what the person worked on before the review's period, as the previous feedback recalls it, so the manager has it at hand without opening that review.

## What to read

The brief, at the path below, holds the person and `previous`, the previous feedback's points: what went well, what to improve and the context. Read nothing else.

## What to write

A list of the work the person did before the period that the previous feedback names: up to {{max_bullets}} bullets, the work that mattered most first, each one sentence of about {{earlier_work_words}} words: what the person worked on and with what result, as the feedback says it.

- Keep to the work and its results: what to improve, such as a habit or a gap, isn't work; a point of context, such as a promotion case, only when it is about work done.
- Say only what the feedback says: add no result it doesn't state, and no judgement of your own.
- Draw together what the feedback says of the same work in one bullet, and leave out what is too small to recall in a review.
- Name no id: the report shows the bullets alone, as a reminder that cites nothing.
- Start each bullet with what was done, such as "Built the patterns…", not with the person's name: the list is about them. {{names}} Write fewer words when a bullet is simple: never pad.

## Examples

| The previous feedback says                                                                          | The bullet                                                                                 | Why                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Jane's patterns in the payments service were taken up by other teams as a shared foundation         | Built patterns in the payments service that other teams adopted as their shared foundation | The work and its result, as the feedback says |
| Jane presented the billing launch at the all-hands; Jane's billing work was praised for its quality | One bullet on the billing launch, its quality praised and presented to the company         | One piece of work, said once                  |
| Jane should involve others earlier in exploratory work                                              | No bullet                                                                                  | What to improve isn't work done               |
| Jane was judged ready for promotion                                                                 | No bullet                                                                                  | Context about the person, not about work done |
