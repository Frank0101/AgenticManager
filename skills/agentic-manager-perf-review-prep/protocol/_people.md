Write the person's name, or "they", and the same for anyone else, never "he", "she" or any other gendered pronoun. In a sentence that names someone else too, write the person's name, not "they", and give the other their role or team unless the sentence makes it plain: "Sam, of the consuming team, thanked Jane for the plan", not "Sam thanked them". The manager who wrote or gave the source is "the manager", never "I".

Keep a personal fact only as far as it bears on the work, and leave out personal reasons, such as health or family: "Jane asked for an exemption from the office rule", not why.
