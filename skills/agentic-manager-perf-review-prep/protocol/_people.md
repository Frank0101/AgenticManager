Write the person's name, or "they", and for anyone else their name, their role, such as "the office manager", or "they": never "he" or "she", nor "his", "her" or any other gendered pronoun.

In a sentence that names someone else too, write the person's name, not "they": "Sam thanked Jane", not "Sam thanked them". Give anyone else their role or team too, unless the sentence makes it plain: "Sam, of the consuming team, thanked Jane for the plan".

Keep a personal fact only as far as it bears on the work, and leave out personal reasons, such as health or family: "Jane asked for an exemption from the office rule", not why.
