# Sponsorship Story

**Use for:** a sponsorship, impact or traction slide: a short pitch on the left, three headline numbers on the right.

**Layout.** Left column: the title, two short paragraphs, a short rule and, at the bottom, a small logo with a web address. Right column: three stat rows between thin rules, each a large number with a circled up-arrow at its right end, then a bold label and two or three lines.

**Style.** Full-bleed muted blue-to-plum gradient with two translucent bubbles at top left, all text white.

**Density.** Medium: about 100 words.
