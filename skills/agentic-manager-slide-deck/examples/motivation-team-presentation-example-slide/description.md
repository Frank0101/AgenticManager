# Success Through Collaboration

**Use for:** a motivational or cultural message: one statement and a short paragraph.

**Layout.** Left: a large uppercase title on two lines, a short rule, a six-line paragraph lower down and a row of small triangles near the bottom. Right: an illustration of five rising bars ending in a big arrow, two figures climbing them and helping each other, which bleeds off the bottom edge. A partial ring sits in the top-right corner.

**Style.** White background, coral title and accents, purple and coral bars.

**Density.** Low: about 50 words.
